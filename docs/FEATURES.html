Version 0.8.6

Achievements

  • 7 new achievements, for 26 in all:
    • Genius: all A's on your midterms and finals
    • Have a Ball: guess who is behind the mask at the masquerade
    • I Can Do Magic Too!: use a relic in a scene
    • Society Secrets: reach the second special meeting of any society
    • I'm Outta Here: make a love interest walk out of a scene
    • And two hidden ones to find

Trips

  • Weekend getaways, booked from the new Trips app on your phone, with friends or more. The pricier the trip, the more affection, trust and desire she feels the moment you set off:
    • A weekend at Brightshore: the beach in swimsuits, the boardwalk at night, a sunrise swim
    • Camping at Fernhollow: a forest trail, a night around the campfire, dawn at the lake
    • A cruise on Jadewater Bay: fly south by magitech airship to a bay of towering karst islands for an overnight cruise on a red-sailed junk, a night in its luxury cabin, and a rowboat into a glowing sea cave
    • An escape to Coralmere Atoll: swim the coral reef, spend the night in an overwater bungalow with a glass floor, and picnic on a sandbar far out in the lagoon, in sarongs
    • A weekend at Saffron Lake: a boat ride past white marble palaces at sunset and dinner on a palace rooftop in saris, then a festival of colors

The Undercroft

  • Your women grow stronger as you grow closer: a new role move once she is your friend and has won a run with you, and her very own ultimate, once a run, once she is your lover. 20 ultimates, one for every woman who fights below.
  • Every meter counts below: affection lands true strikes, trust gives her more footing, and desire fills a passion gauge that empowers her next move.
  • Enemies show their next move, so you can guard, ward or taunt before a heavy blow lands.
  • The deeper you go, the harder they fight: armor, heavy blows, summons, healing and silences, and guardians that change as you wear them down.
  • Rebalanced from top to bottom: the first two depths are yours the week they open, the middle ones are a real fight, and the last two are for a party of lovers only.
  • Echoes: a post-game mode once every anchor is set. Never the same twice, built from the depths and their enemies, and stronger than anything before it.
  • Adventurer outfits: every woman who clears an Echo with you earns her own fantasy outfit to wear on her days, from Daphne's paladin plate to Tink's thief's leathers. Switch it on or off from her page in the Friends app.

Art

  • Swimsuits for every love interest, with a pregnant set.

  • New places: 2, with 6 animated views
