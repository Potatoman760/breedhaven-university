[SIZE=5][B]Overview[/B][/SIZE]

Breedhaven is a modern kingdom of trams, phones and magic, where every woman works some spell
and only women can. Seven girls are born for every boy, and only one man in ten can father a child.

You have just been measured a [B]True Sire[/B]: the rare man with whom a woman always conceives.
Now you are starting your first year at Breedhaven University, in the capital, Seedblossom,
and everyone on campus knows exactly what you are.

Twenty women, from classmates and roommates to professors, a slime girl, an orc and the newly crowned queen,
all have their own lives, schedules and secrets. What happens with each of them is up to you.

[SIZE=5][B]How it plays[/B][/SIZE]

Every conversation is written live by the LLM you point the game at. You type what you say, and she answers in
character. You aren't picking from a list of replies.

Everything around the conversations is ordinary game logic: the calendar, your money and stats, classes and grades,
what each woman will and won't do, pregnancy, children and the house you build for them.
